# Parure La Plee

Style is more fun when it's shared.

Parure La Plee is an AI personal stylist app. It finds your colour season, organises your closet and styles outfits from clothes you already own. Follow people whose style you love, share your best looks, and shop pre-loved pieces, all filtered to your own colour season.

Free to start, with a 14-day Premium trial. Available on [iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/parure-la-plee/id6784032743) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.parurelaplee.app).

## What you can do

- **Find your colour season** from one selfie, and get a colour card with your best shades.
- **Build a digital closet.** Photograph what you own; backgrounds are removed and every piece is tagged for your colouring.
- **Get dressed for 45 occasions** (brunch, school pickup, a wedding, a job interview) with complete looks from your own pieces. Premium.
- **Plan your week** on an outfit calendar with the weather, and pack for trips with the Getaway Edit. The Getaway Edit is Premium.
- **Join the Style Circle**, a community where you can follow friends and stylists. Browsing is free; posting looks and asking for advice is Premium.
- **Buy and sell pre-loved** in Parure Preloved. Buying is free; selling is Premium, and Parure takes 8%.
- **Work with a stylist.** Invite an approved stylist or personal shopper into your wardrobe. Premium.

## Sustainability first

The average person wears only 20% of their wardrobe. Parure La Plee helps you wear the other 80%. Every outfit recommendation starts with what you already own; shopping suggestions come second, and only when they complete the look.
