  • The referred user has not cancelled their subscription before or during the first billing cycle. If a referred user cancels their subscription at any point during the 14-day free trial or before their first payment is successfully processed, no commission is earned or owed to the Creator for that referral.

When commissions are paid — payout schedule and minimums
