# For stylists and personal shoppers: Parure La Plee Trusted Partners

Work inside your clients' wardrobes. Earn as you grow.

A curated partnership for verified style professionals: fashion stylists, personal shoppers, photographers, makeup artists, wardrobe consultants and other qualified professionals whose work involves creating, coordinating or influencing complete looks for clients. Approved Trusted Partners join the Parure La Plee Style Team.

## How it works

- **Work inside a client's wardrobe, remotely.** Clients invite you into their closet. Build looks from what they already own, from any city, and send them for approval.
- **Add linked items for what's missing.** Spot a gap and attach a linked piece to the look. Your client sees the exact item and where to buy it.
- **Tiered commission for every referral.** Earn 15 to 25% on every Premium subscriber you refer, tiered by referral performance, for the life of their membership.
- **0% fees.** Parure takes nothing from the styling services you charge your clients.

## Who can become a Trusted Partner?

- Works professionally in styling, fashion, beauty, photography or a related field that contributes to complete client looks
- Provides evidence of professional work, portfolio, business or client experience
- Style, fashion, beauty or lifestyle expertise
- Genuine professional or audience engagement
- Alignment with Parure La Plee's approach to personal colour intelligence and intentional style
- 5,000+ followers can be considered, but a following is not required

Anne reviews every application personally. Response within 5 to 7 business days.
