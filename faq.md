# Parure La Plee FAQ

## Is Parure La Plee free?

Yes. The closet, calendar, lookbooks, browsing the Style Circle and your colour season with a few of your best shades are free. Premium adds your full colour palette, AI outfits, your personalised AI model, bulk upload, the Getaway Edit, posting in the Style Circle, stylist and personal shopper access, and selling in Parure Preloved, with a 14-day free trial.

## Is it only for women?

No. Styling and language adapt to your gender identity, body type and pronouns, and everyone gets the same closet tools.

## Do I have to upload a selfie?

Only for colour analysis. You can build your closet and plan outfits without one. Your selfie is encrypted, and you can delete it at any time.

## Is there a minimum age?

Yes. Parure La Plee is for adults aged 18 and over, and you confirm you are 18 or over when you sign up. Adding your birthday in Settings is optional. You can delete your account and data at any time from the app.

## Which phones does it work on?

Android through [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.parurelaplee.app), and iPhone through the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/parure-la-plee/id6784032743).

## How much does Premium cost?

$12.99 per month or $119.00 per year, with a 14-day free trial. See [Pricing](https://parure.app/pricing.md).
