You don't need more clothes. You need to know what's actually missing.
From Anne

I used to buy things I already owned — a slightly different black cardigan, another version of the same white shirt — because I couldn't picture what I actually had. What I really needed wasn't more clothes, it was to understand the two or three real gaps in my closet so I could shop on purpose instead of on impulse. That's a much harder problem than "recommend more stuff," and it's the one I most wanted this app to solve.

— Anne
Feature Sneak Peek
Gap detection, not a shopping feed.

Once the app knows your wardrobe, it can tell you what's actually missing — a specific gap, not an endless scroll of suggestions. Think "you don't have a neutral layering piece for this outfit" instead of a wall of things you didn't ask for.

Style Notes — Seasonal Trend

Early September is when back-to-school and back-to-routine energy starts creeping into everyone's closet, whether or not it applies to you. It's a good week to be honest about outfits you wanted to wear this summer but couldn't quite piece together — that's useful information, not a failure.
