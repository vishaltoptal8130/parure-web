From Anne

People sometimes ask what Parure La Plee is “like” — is it Pinterest, is it a shopping app, is it a stylist. Here’s the honest answer: I want it to feel like a friend with impeccable taste going through your closet with you, not a website trying to sell you something. No infinite scroll, no pressure to buy. Just your own clothes, put together in ways you hadn’t thought of.

— Anne
Feature Sneak Peek
Outfits built from what you own.

Once your digital wardrobe exists, the app generates complete outfits from your own closet — tuned to your colour season and the occasion you pick. It’s the feature people react to most, because it isn’t inspiration from a stranger’s closet. It’s yours, styled back to you.

Style Notes — Seasonal Trend

Mid-August is transition season in disguise — still warm, but the light and the mood start turning toward autumn. Notice which pieces in your keep pile could believably go both ways. Those are worth building an autumn wardrobe around later.

Get Closet-Ready
Step 3 of 6

From your keep pile, find your five most-worn pieces of the summer — the ones you reach for without thinking. Set them aside. These are your hero pieces, and they’ll be the first things worth building outfits around on day one.
