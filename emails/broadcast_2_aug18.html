"I have nothing to wear" — while standing in front of a full closet. Sound familiar?
From Anne

Before Parure La Plee was an app, it was a question I couldn't stop asking myself: why did I feel like I had nothing to wear when my closet was completely full? The answer, it turns out, wasn't that I owned the wrong things. It was that I'd never actually seen my wardrobe as a whole — just as a pile of individual pieces I'd forgotten how to combine. That's the gap we're building to close.

— Anne
Feature Sneak Peek
Your whole closet, finally visible.

The app's digital wardrobe turns your closet into something you can actually see and search — organized by category, color, and season instead of buried on a hanger. A guided flow walks you through eight categories one at a time, celebrating progress along the way, building toward a 65-piece foundation. It's the foundation everything else is built on, from outfit generation to gap detection.

Style Notes — Seasonal Trend

Mid-August is when a lot of closets are quietly holding pieces that never get worn simply because they've never been seen next to the right partner piece. Before you touch anything, just notice: is there something in there you haven't worn all summer, and can you say why?

Get Closet-Ready
Step 2 of 7

Time to sort. Pull everything from that same shelf or drawer and make three quick piles: keep, worth listing, and unsure. That middle pile isn't a donation box — pieces that no longer fit your life might be exactly what someone else's color season is looking for on the marketplace we're building. Your keep pile is what you'll photograph for your wardrobe upload on day one. Don't overthink the unsure pile — it's allowed to exist.
