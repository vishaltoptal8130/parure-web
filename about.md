# About Parure La Plee

> "Your wardrobe is already full of possibility. We help you see it."

I love clothes. I love shopping, I love fashion, and I still found myself standing in a wardrobe full of beautiful clothes feeling completely uninspired. Something would come together, but never quite all the way. Parure La Plee is the answer I built for myself, and for everyone who has ever felt that same quiet frustration.

Anne Harrison, Founder and CEO, Parure La Plee, Orlando, Florida.

## What Parure La Plee stands for

- **Personal colour intelligence.** Every recommendation is filtered to your own colour season.
- **Sustainability first.** Outfits start with what you already own. The marketplace gives pieces you no longer wear a second life.
- **Style is more fun when it's shared.** The Style Circle connects you with friends and stylists, and the Style Team lets a professional work inside your wardrobe.
