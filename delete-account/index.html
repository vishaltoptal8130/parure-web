Account & Data Deletion Request

Google Play & App Store compliance guidelines

You are in control of your personal data. Delete your account yourself in the app, or ask us to do it by email.

Method A · Instant

Delete directly in the app

  1. Open the Parure La Plee mobile app.
  2. Go to Settings (gear icon).
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and associated data are deleted immediately.
Method B · Email

Request it by email

If you no longer have access to the mobile app, or prefer to ask on the web, send a deletion request to our support team from your registered email address.

Go to the request form

What gets deleted

When we process your request, the following data tied to your account is permanently removed from our servers and database.

  • Account credentials & profileEmail address, user ID, full name, avatar, bio, age/DOB preferences, and auth sessions.
  • Digital wardrobe & closet itemsUploaded item images, categories, tags, color analysis data, and personal closet records.
  • Outfits & styling dataSaved outfits, lookbooks, travel packing lists, and mood boards.
  • Social & Style Circle activityCreated forum posts, comments, likes, saved posts, and followers/following relationships.
  • Selfie & colour analysis data (if applicable)Your colour analysis selfie, AI likeness images, and related colour profile fields.
Retention & exceptions. All personal profile and user-generated data are deleted permanently. In rare circumstances, minimal anonymized transactional records (such as App Store / Google Play subscription receipt tokens) may be retained for up to 30 days solely to fulfill legal compliance, tax reporting, or fraud prevention requirements as required by law.

Submit a web deletion request

Send an email from your registered account email address with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" to support@parurelaplee.com or parureapp@gmail.com.
