Account & Data Deletion Request

Google Play & App Store Compliance Guidelines
01

How to Delete Your Account

At Parure La Plee, you have full control over your personal data and account settings. You can request or perform account deletion using either of the following methods:

Method A: Directly In-App (Instant)
1. Open the Parure La Plee Mobile App.
2. Go to Settings (gear icon).
3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
4. Confirm account deletion. Your account and associated data will be deleted immediately.
Method B: Web Request (Email)
If you no longer have access to the mobile app or wish to request data deletion via web, you can submit a deletion request to our support team using your registered email address.
02

Data That Will Be Deleted

Upon processing your deletion request, the following user data associated with your account will be permanently removed from our servers and database:

  • Account Credentials & Profile: Email address, user ID, full name, avatar, bio, age/DOB preferences, and auth sessions.
  • Digital Wardrobe & Closet Items: Uploaded item images, categories, tags, color analysis data, and personal closet records.
  • Outfits & Styling Data: Saved outfits, lookbooks, travel packing lists, and mood boards.
  • Social & Community Activity: Created forum posts, comments, likes, saved posts, and followers/following relationships.
  • Biometric Data (if applicable): Any uploaded selfie images used solely for local/cloud color season analysis.
03

Data Retention & Exceptions

All personal profile and user-generated data are deleted permanently. In rare circumstances, minimal anonymized transactional records (such as App Store / Google Play subscription receipt tokens) may be retained for up to 30 days solely to fulfill legal compliance, tax reporting, or fraud prevention requirements as required by law.

Submit a Web Deletion Request

To request account deletion via web, send an email from your registered account email address with the subject line "Account Deletion Request" to:

support@parurelaplee.com  or  parureapp@gmail.com
