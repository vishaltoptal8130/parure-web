Parure La Plee is operated by S9 Enterprises LLC d/b/a Parure La Plee ("Parure," "we," "us," or "our"). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your name, image, or likeness in Parure advertising or marketing without separate permission.
01

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, information collected automatically when you use our Services, and information from third parties.

Information you provide:

  • Account information — name, email address, password, and authentication information when you register or sign in.
  • Profile information — style preferences, body measurements, apparel sizing, gender or presentation preferences, preferred fit, coverage preferences, color season, and other profile information you choose to provide.
  • Photos and images — selfies used for color analysis, likeness-based features, and profile images; wardrobe and garment photos; marketplace images; and other images you choose to provide to features such as event analysis, shopping scans, or moodboards.
  • Color-analysis data — characteristics derived from your selfie for purposes of generating your personal color analysis and Comprehensive Personal Color Profile ("CPCP"), including skin tone and undertone, skin depth, hair color and tone, eye color characteristics, color-season classification, confidence information, and associated color-palette data. See Section 06 for additional information.
  • Body and fit information — measurements, apparel sizes, body-shape information, fit preferences, and related information you choose to provide for styling and AI-generated outfit features.
  • Payment and transaction information — payment information is processed through our payment providers. We do not store full payment-card numbers. We may maintain transaction, subscription, marketplace, receipt, refund, and payout information associated with your account.
  • Communications — messages and other information you send to our support team or otherwise provide to us.
  • Waitlist information — information such as your email address submitted through parure.app before launch.
  • Usage and planning information — outfits worn, occasions selected, wardrobe information, cost-per-wear information, trips, and calendar events you choose to sync or provide.

Information collected automatically:

  • Usage data — features you access, outfits you generate, marketplace activity, and actions taken in the Services.
  • Device information — device type, operating system, app version, device or advertising identifiers where permitted, and similar technical information.
  • Location information — when you grant permission, location information used to provide weather-based recommendations. Coordinates sent to our weather provider are coarsened before transmission.
  • Log and diagnostic information — IP address, access times, crash information, performance information, and similar technical data.
  • Analytics and attribution information — information relating to use of the Services, subscriptions, and advertising attribution, subject to applicable permissions and consent requirements.

06

Color analysis, facial image data, and consent

How the color analysis works

If you choose to use Parure La Plee's color-analysis feature, you provide a selfie that is analyzed to generate your personalized color analysis and Comprehensive Personal Color Profile ("CPCP").

The color-analysis process evaluates color-related characteristics from regions of the image associated with your skin, hair, and eyes. Depending on the analysis, these characteristics may include skin tone, undertone and depth, hair tone and depth, and eye color characteristics such as hue, saturation, and warmth.

These characteristics are evaluated using a weighted color-classification process to generate information such as your color-season classification, a confidence score, and associated color-palette information. This information is then used to provide personalized color, wardrobe, outfit, and shopping recommendations.

What the color analysis does not do

Parure's CPCP color-analysis technology is not designed to identify or authenticate you based on your face.

The CPCP color-analysis process:

  • does not use facial recognition to determine your identity or match you against an identity database;
  • does not measure or map your facial geometry for identification or authentication;
  • does not create a faceprint or face-geometry template for identifying you in another image; and
  • uses the characteristics derived from your selfie to generate color-analysis and styling information.

Some privacy laws impose additional requirements on technologies that process facial images or biometric identifiers. We handle facial-image and color-analysis data in accordance with applicable privacy laws and provide notices and obtain consent where required for the processing performed.

Third-party AI processing

When you request color analysis, your selfie is transmitted to Anthropic's Claude AI service for analysis. The image and instructions relating to the requested analysis are provided to Anthropic to generate characteristics used for your color-season analysis and CPCP.

Parure stores your selfie and derived profile information through its backend and storage infrastructure, including Supabase.

Your choice and consent

Before processing your selfie for color analysis, we provide notice regarding the processing and obtain your affirmative consent where required by applicable law. Consent is not preselected or assumed.

You may decline to use the color-analysis feature and continue using other available portions of the Services that do not require color analysis.

Retention and deletion

Parure retains your selfie and associated color-analysis information while necessary to provide the applicable features and for the purposes disclosed at collection.

You may withdraw your consent or request deletion of your selfie and associated color-analysis information. The Services are designed so that withdrawal of the applicable consent deletes the stored selfie, generated likeness-based outfit images, and related profile fields and records the withdrawal of consent.

Parure's systems are also designed to automatically delete stored selfies and generated likeness-based outfit images six (6) months after they are created, even if you have not withdrawn consent.

Deletion from third-party providers is subject to the applicable provider's processing and retention practices. We do not represent that deletion from Parure's systems automatically constitutes deletion from a third-party provider unless that provider's applicable contractual terms and retention practices provide for such deletion.
