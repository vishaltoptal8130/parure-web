01

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, information collected automatically when you use our services, and information from third parties.

Information you provide:

  • Account information — name, email address, and password when you register
  • Profile information — style preferences, body measurements, and sizing details you choose to enter
  • Photos and images — selfies used for color analysis and photos of wardrobe items you upload
  • Facial characteristic data — skin tone, hair color, and eye color values derived from your selfie. See Section 05 for how we treat this data and obtain your consent
  • Payment information — processed securely by Stripe; we do not store full card numbers
  • Communications — messages you send to our support team
  • Waitlist information — email address submitted via parure.app before launch
  • Usage patterns — outfits worn, occasions selected, cost-per-wear data, and calendar events you choose to sync, used to personalize your AI recommendations over time

Information collected automatically:

  • Usage data — features you access, outfits you generate, and actions taken in the app
  • Device information — device type, operating system, and app version
  • Location data — approximate location used for weather-based outfit recommendations (only when you grant permission)
  • Log data — IP address, access times, and pages or screens viewed

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to provide, improve, and personalise the Parure La Plee experience.

  • Generate your personal colour season analysis and Comprehensive Personal Colour Profile (CPCP) using AI
  • Generate your personal avatar and AI outfit previews that depict your likeness
  • Build and style AI outfit recommendations from your digital wardrobe across 42 occasions
  • Process subscription payments and manage your account
  • Send you transactional emails — receipts, security alerts, and product updates
  • Send marketing communications, with your consent (you may opt out at any time)
  • Improve our AI models and recommendation quality using anonymised, aggregated data
  • Detect fraud and ensure platform security
  • Comply with legal obligations
We do not use your photos, facial characteristic data, or likeness to train AI models that are shared externally, sold to advertisers, or used for any purpose beyond providing you with the Parure La Plee service. We do not use your face or likeness in advertising, marketing, or promotional materials without your separate written permission.

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — third-party vendors who help us operate the app, including Supabase (database), Stripe (payments), RevenueCat (subscriptions), Cloudinary (image storage), Mixpanel (analytics), and Anthropic (AI processing). 05

Photo and image data

Your photos are central to the Parure La Plee experience. We take their handling seriously.

  • Selfies you upload for color analysis are processed by our AI and stored securely in your account. They are never shared with other users or sold to any third party. They are processed by our AI provider (Anthropic) and stored by our image storage provider (Cloudinary), both of whom are contractually restricted to processing on our behalf.
  • Wardrobe item photos are stored in your personal digital wardrobe and are visible only to you, unless you choose to list an item in the marketplace or share a look.
  • You may delete any photo from your account at any time. Deletion from our storage typically occurs within 30 days.
  • You may delete your selfie and your derived facial characteristic data independently of the rest of your account. See Section 05.
06

Biometric data and your consent

Because our color analysis works by examining your face, some of the data we derive may qualify as biometric information under laws including the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA), the Texas Capture or Use of Biometric Identifier Act (CUBI), and the Washington My Health My Data Act. We want to be completely clear about what we do and do not do.

What we collect and why. When you upload a selfie for color analysis, our AI examines the image to determine your skin tone, hair color, and eye color. From these values we generate your seasonal color profile — for example, Warm Autumn or True Winter — and your Comprehensive Personal Color Profile (CPCP). The sole purpose of this processing is to provide you with personalized color and styling recommendations within the Parure La Plee app.

What we do NOT do.

  • We do not use facial recognition to identify you, match you against any database, or determine who you are
  • We do not create or store a faceprint, face template, face geometry map, or any identifier capable of recognizing you in another image
  • We do not sell, lease, trade, or otherwise profit from your biometric or facial characteristic data
  • We do not disclose your biometric or facial characteristic data to any third party except our processing and storage providers acting on our behalf, or where required by law
  • We do not use your facial data to train AI models that serve anyone other than you
Your consent. Before we process your selfie for color analysis, we ask for your explicit, affirmative consent through a separate in-app disclosure. You must actively agree — consent is never assumed, never bundled into another agreement, and never pre-checked. You may decline and continue using Parure La Plee's free wardrobe features without providing a selfie. If you decline, we do not collect facial characteristic data of any kind.

Retention and destruction. We retain your selfie and derived facial characteristic data only as long as your account remains active and you continue to use color-dependent features. We will permanently destroy this data at the earliest of: (a) when you withdraw consent or delete the data in your settings; (b) when you delete your account; or (c) within three (3) years of your last interaction with the app. Destruction occurs within 30 days of the triggering event.

Withdrawing consent. You may withdraw your biometric consent at any time in your account settings or by emailing us. When you withdraw consent, we permanently delete your selfie and derived facial characteristic data. Your saved color profile will no longer be available and color-dependent features will be disabled, but the rest of your account and wardrobe remain intact.

Your likeness, avatar, and AI-generated images

Parure La Plee can generate a personal avatar that depicts your likeness wearing outfits from your wardrobe. This is a meaningful use of your image, and you should know exactly how it works.

  • How your avatar is made. Your avatar is generated from the selfie you provide and any measurements you enter. It is created solely to show you how outfits may look on you.
  • Who can see it. Your avatar and AI-generated outfit images are visible only to you, unless you choose to share them using the app's sharing features.
  • You own your likeness. Generating an avatar does not transfer any rights in your likeness to us. You retain all rights in your own image and likeness.
  • We will not use your likeness in marketing without permission. We will never use your face, avatar, or AI-generated images of you in advertising, promotional material, social media, press, or App Store content unless you have given separate, specific, written permission — for example, by signing a media release. Agreeing to this Privacy Policy is not such permission.
  • Deletion. When you delete your selfie, withdraw biometric consent, or delete your account, your avatar and the AI-generated images derived from it are deleted as well.

Sharing features and your likeness

Parure La Plee includes features that let you share your color palette card, your styled looks, and your outfits — inside the app's community or outside it on social platforms.

  • Sharing is always your choice. Nothing that depicts you is shared automatically or by default.

Data retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services.

  • Account data is retained until you delete your account
  • Wardrobe and outfit data is retained until you delete individual items or your account
  • Selfies and derived facial characteristic data are retained under the specific rules in Section 05 — destroyed on consent withdrawal, on account deletion, or within three years of your last interaction, whichever comes first

  • Payment records are retained for seven years to comply with tax and financial regulations

  • Anonymized, aggregated analytics data may be retained indefinitely

When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where we are required by law to retain it.

Children's and minors' privacy

Parure La Plee is not directed to children and is intended for users aged 18 and over.

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 under any circumstances.
  • We do not knowingly collect selfies, facial characteristic data, or biometric information from anyone under the age of 18. Because biometric laws provide heightened protection for minors, our color analysis feature is intended for adults.
  • If a user between 13 and 17 uses Parure La Plee, a parent or legal guardian must consent on their behalf, and biometric consent must be given by that parent or guardian.
  • If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, or biometric data from a minor, please contact us immediately at parureapp@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

Data security

We implement industry-standard technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

  • All data is transmitted using TLS encryption
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • Payment data is handled exclusively by Stripe, which is PCI-DSS compliant
  • Images, including selfies, are stored in encrypted cloud storage
  • Facial characteristic data is stored with at least the same degree of protection we apply to other confidential and sensitive information, and no less than a reasonable standard of care for our industry
  • Access to your data within our team is limited to those who need it to operate the service

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, please contact us immediately.
