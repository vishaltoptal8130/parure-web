Start free. Unlock your stylist.

Organise your closet, plan outfits and join the Style Circle.

- Unlimited digital closet
- Background removal on photos
- Outfit calendar and history log
- Wishlists, moodboards and lookbooks
- Browse the Style Circle and buy pre-loved

## Premium: $12.99 per month or $119.00 per year

Includes a 14-day free trial. The yearly plan saves 24% (about $9.92 a month). Your AI stylist, full colour palette and a style team on call.

Everything in Free, plus:

- Full colour analysis and palette
- AI outfits for 45 occasions
- Bulk upload, up to 10 pieces at a time
- Getaway Edit AI packing
- Post and ask for advice in the Style Circle
- Personalised AI model
- Outfit gap detection and shopping recommendations
- Calendar sync with outfit suggestions
- Stylist and personal shopper access (Trusted Partners)
- Sell in Parure Preloved (Parure takes 8%)
- Share moodboards and lookbooks with friends to collaborate
- Priority support

Prices are in US dollars as shown on parure.app. Subscriptions are managed through the App Store or Google Play.
