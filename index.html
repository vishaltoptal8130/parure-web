How Parure La Plee works

Style that knows you

Four steps from selfie to styled. Parure La Plee does the work — you just get dressed.

1
Upload your selfie
Parure La Plee analyzes your skin tone, hair colour, and eye colour to determine your unique seasonal colour profile — Warm Autumn, Cool Summer, and more.
2
Build your digital closet
Every item is tagged with your colour profile, so you know at a glance what's a Best, Complementary, or worth Styling with purpose.
3
Get styled for any occasion
Choose from 45 occasions styled from what you already own — brunch, date night, concerts, weddings, work, and more. Parure La Plee builds a complete look from your closet, with AI photo preview.
