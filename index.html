Questions.

Something we didn't cover? Our support page has more answers and a way to reach us.

Visit support
Is Parure La Plee free?

Yes. The closet, calendar, lookbooks and feed are free. Premium adds colour analysis and AI outfits, with a 14-day free trial.

Is it only for women?

No. Styling and language adapt to your gender identity, body type and pronouns, and everyone gets the same closet tools.

Do I have to upload a selfie?

Only for colour analysis. You can build your closet and plan outfits without one. Your selfie is encrypted, and you can delete it at any time.

What happens to my birth date?

It's stored securely and used only for age verification. You can delete your account and data at any time from the app.

Which phones does it work on?

Android through Google Play, and iPhone through the App Store.
