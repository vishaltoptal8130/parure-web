# How Parure La Plee works

From selfie to styled: five steps from one photo of your face to a week of outfits from clothes you already own.

## 1. Upload one selfie

Parure La Plee reads your skin tone, hair and eye colour and finds your colour season. Use natural light, a bare face and face the camera. Your selfie is used only for colour analysis, it is encrypted, and you can delete it at any time.

## 2. Get your colour card

Your season and a few of your best shades are free. Premium unlocks the full palette in three groups: Best for you, Complementary, and Style with purpose. Share your colour card with friends or your stylist.

## 3. Fill your closet

Add what you own and the background is removed for you. Every piece is tagged for your colouring, and the app tells you what you haven't worn this season. Premium adds bulk upload, up to 10 pieces at a time.

## 4. Pick an occasion

Choose from 45 occasions, such as Sunday brunch, wedding guest, first date, school pickup, concert, gallery opening, cruise and job interview. With Premium you get complete looks from your own pieces and a preview on your personalised AI model. Swap, add or remove any piece in a tap.

## 5. Plan your week

Put looks on your outfit calendar, check the forecast, and pack for a trip with the Getaway Edit (Premium). Tomorrow's outfit is already decided.

## No stylist needed

You don't need a stylist, a shopper or a free hour. Add your closet once, open the app each morning to a look for the weather and your colours, then wear it, swap a piece, or ask for another.
